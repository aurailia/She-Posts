She Posts Assistant

Privacy & terms · Last updated November 22, 2026

The short version: everything stays on your own computer. She Posts Assistant doesn't send your information to us or to anyone else. We don't collect it, sell it or share it.

What it does

She Posts Assistant is a Chrome extension that helps you post your promos from She Posts into the Skool groups you belong to, keep track of where you've posted, and see how your posts are doing.

What it reads, and why

  • Your week from She Posts. When you tap Send to Assistant, your posts, images and group list are passed to the extension so it can fill them into Skool.
  • Skool pages you're signed in to. It fills in your post in the Skool post box, and posts it only when you tap Post it or Send to your checked groups.
  • Analytics is off until you turn it on. Everything below about your posts, your communities and your referrals only happens after you check the box and tap Turn on analytics.
  • How your posts are doing. It reads the likes and comments on posts you made with it, and the names of people who liked or commented, so you can see your warm people and reply to comments.
  • Your own communities. If you run a Skool community, it reads your member list to show new members and when they joined, and the communities listed on their public Skool profiles.
  • Your referral earnings, if you choose. This is a separate checkbox. It reads the amounts on your Skool Affiliates page: what you've earned, what's ready to pay out, which groups pay a commission, and the names of people who joined through your links. It keeps only the amounts, names and profile pictures. It never keeps your bank, payout account or tax details.

It only reads what Skool already shows you when you're signed in. It never sees or stores your Skool password.

Where your information is kept

Everything is saved in Chrome's storage on your own computer, and in She Posts' storage in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server we run, because we don't run one. The only website it talks to is Skool, the same way your browser does when you use Skool yourself.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share your information.
  • We don't use analytics, ads or tracking.
  • We don't use your information for anything other than the features above.

Removing your information

To stop analytics or referrals, tap Turn off analytics or uncheck referral earnings, in the Assistant panel or on the Analytics page. Turning something off deletes what it saved for it.

Tap Clear everything at the bottom of the Assistant panel, or remove the extension from Chrome. Either one deletes what it saved on your computer.

Using She Posts and the Assistant

By using She Posts and She Posts Assistant, you agree to the following.

Keeping your groups up to date

Every Skool group sets its own rules: promo days, how many posts a day, categories and membership levels. Groups change these whenever they want.

  • The starter groups and categories are there to help you get going. They may not match a group's current rules.
  • It's your responsibility to check each group's rules and keep your level, promo days, posts a day and category up to date in She Posts.
  • We're not responsible for changes a group makes, for posts that land on the wrong day or in the wrong category, or for posts a group removes.

What you post

  • You're responsible for everything you post, and for following each group's rules and Skool's terms.
  • The Assistant only posts when you tap Post it or Send to your checked groups. Look over your post before you send it.
  • Please post at a human pace, only where your level allows. Groups can remove members who post too much or in the wrong places.

Referral links

The "Join here" links on the starter groups are Aurailia's referral links. If you join a paid level through one of them, Aurailia may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you.

Not made by Skool

She Posts and She Posts Assistant are made by Aurailia. They aren't made by, connected to or endorsed by Skool.

No guarantees

  • The tools are provided as they are. Skool can change its site at any time, and when it does, some features may stop working until they're updated.

  • We're not responsible for any loss of posts, data or results from using the tools.

Your backups

Everything lives on your own computer, so if you clear your browser or switch computers, it can be lost. Use Back up now in the Assistant once a week, and keep the file somewhere safe.
